Why BillBuddy?

Stop Losing Money to Expired Warranties

Every year, millions of consumers pay out-of-pocket for gadget repairs simply because they lost the receipt or forgot the warranty expired last week. BillBuddy fixes that.

❌ The Painful Traditional Way
  • 📄 Faded thermal receipts: Paper invoices fade into blank sheets within 6 months.
  • 🔍 Lost receipts: Searching through folders, shoe boxes, or deleted emails during emergencies.
  • ⌛ Unknown expiry: No reminder until your appliance breaks down and the service center rejects the claim.
  • 💸 Unnecessary repair costs: Paying ₹5,000 - ₹20,000 for repairs that were rightfully under warranty.
✨ The Smart BillBuddy Way
  • ☁️ Supabase Cloud Backup: Your PDF bills and receipts are safely encrypted and permanently accessible.
  • ⏳ Automatic Countdown: Live days-remaining calculation from day 1 to warranty expiry.
  • ⚡ Instant Search: Find your iPhone, TV, or Fridge invoice in 2 seconds with brand and category search.
  • 🔒 Firebase Google Auth: One-click secure sign-in. No passwords to remember.
